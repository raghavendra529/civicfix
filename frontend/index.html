Automated Multi-Class Classification

7 Municipal Infrastructure Defect Classes

Our model was specifically calibrated on verified urban municipal benchmark datasets to eliminate false positives and distinguish physical structural failures from benign conditions.

1. Potholes

Depressions and craters in asphalt bitumen posing acute vehicular hazard. Dispatches P1 road patching crew.

Priority: P1 High

2. Road Cracks

Transverse, longitudinal, and fatigue alligator fractures requiring preventive asphalt sealant maintenance.

Priority: P3 Routine

3. Damaged Manhole

Displaced, sunken, or shattered cast-iron utility and stormwater lids presenting critical pedestrian fall hazards.

Priority: P1 Urgent

4. Roadside Garbage

Illegal solid waste dumping, overflowing public receptacles, and sidewalk litter obstructing drainage.

Priority: P3 Medium

5. Broken Streetlight

Physically fractured or leaning poles, shattered luminaire casings, and hazardous exposed electrical wiring.

Priority: P2 High

6. Pipeline Leakage

Surface water main bursts, road sub-base pooling, and joint seepage causing foundation erosion.

Priority: P1 Critical

7. Normal Pavement

Clean, undamaged asphalt and concrete road surface meeting municipal safety criteria.

Priority: P4 Safe
